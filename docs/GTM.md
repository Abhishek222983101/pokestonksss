# Go-to-market (Colosseum "Business Plan")

**Product:** a real-time, provably-fair creature battler with zero-friction onboarding (guest play, no wallet, no server) — a consumer showcase of MagicBlock's rollup.

**Why it can grow**
- **Share loop:** every duel is a link/QR that joins in two taps → organic invites; shareable result cards.
- **Collect loop:** BrokerDex (defeat each species), Shrimp→Whale ladder, daily streaks.
- **Trust loop:** every roll has a receipt; any match is replayable on `/verify` — fairness is the brand.

**Demand validation (next 30 days):** ship to MagicBlock Forge users; weekly "Fair" seasons with community leaderboards; measure D1/D7 retention and invite conversion.

**Business model:** sponsored routes (protocols pay for a themed route/booth and mascot), cosmetic packs, and the sealed-turn + VRF-receipt engine as a licensable library for other on-chain games. No wagering, no token.

**Distribution:** Solana Mobile/Seeker, Telegram mini-app wrapper, Superteam/Colosseum communities, MagicBlock Forge + Quests.
